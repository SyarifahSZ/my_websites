The Story of Velora Perfume
The journey of Velora Perfume began with our founder, Puan Natasha, and her deep passion for the fresh, enchanting scents of local flora.Inspired by traditional blooms such as Kenanga(Cananga odorata), Kesidang (Vallaris glabra), Bunga Cina (Gardenia jasminoides) and Sundal Malam (Polianthes tuberosa).
She noticed a gap in the market: these beautiful scents were rarely captured in perfumes because their natural fragrance is notoriously delicate and fleeting.Driven by a desire to preserve these scents, Velora utilizes cutting-edge technology and "floral dissection"to study the molecular origin of each aroma.
This scientific approach allows us to extract the purest essence of the flowers, resulting in high-qualitylong-lasting fragrances. Our mission is to provide premium local scents that are accessible and affordable for everyone, regardless of age or gender.
